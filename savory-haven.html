Our Story

A Neighborhood Table Where Seasons Set the Menu

Founded in 2008 by Chef Marco Rivera, Deddies Haven was born from a simple belief: great food doesn't need to be complicated—it just needs to be honest. We source directly from local farms and let the season's finest ingredients guide every dish.

Our mission is to create a warm, welcoming space where food brings people together. From our wood-fired kitchen to your table, every detail is crafted with intention.

  • Farm-to-Table Fresh

    Ingredients sourced weekly from local farms within 50 miles.

  • Wood-Fired Mastery

    Our signature open kitchen uses traditional wood-fire techniques.

  • Cooked with Passion

    15 years of family recipes perfected by award-winning chefs.
